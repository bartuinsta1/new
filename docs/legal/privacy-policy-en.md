# Masalcık Privacy Policy (English)

> DRAFT — to be reviewed by counsel before launch, especially before launching in the US (COPPA) or the EU/UK (GDPR,
> UK Age Appropriate Design Code). Published at https://masalcik.app/en/privacy.

**Who we are:** [Company name, address] ("Masalcık", "we"). Contact: destek@masalcik.app

Masalcık is a bedtime story app for children aged 3–9. **A parent or guardian sets the app up.** Children never create
accounts, enter contact details or see purchases or external links: these are all behind a parental gate.

## What we collect
- **About your child (only what you enter):** first name (and optional pronunciation), age, interests, optional
  "values" topics, chosen avatar and companion character, stories they listened to and the story text, stickers earned.
  We never collect a child's photo, voice, location, contact details or device identifiers.
- **About you:** an anonymous account ID tied to the device, bedtime and app settings, subscription status (product,
  trial, expiry, App Store transaction ID), invite code. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
- **Only if you choose voice cloning (Premium):** a ~1-minute recording of your voice and the voice model made from it,
  with separate, explicit consent.
- **Anonymous usage events** (e.g. "story finished", "onboarding step"), not linked to any person, never used for ads.

## How we use it
Only to provide the service: generating, checking and narrating personal stories, the library and rewards, verifying
your subscription and free-tier limits, and invite rewards. We do **not** show ads, build profiles, track children
across apps or websites, or sell or share personal data.

## Service providers
Data is processed on our behalf by: **Supabase** (database and servers, [region]); **Anthropic** (Claude AI model — the
child's first name, age, interests and story settings are sent to write the story; Anthropic does not train models on
API data); **Microsoft Azure** (text-to-speech of the story text, [region]); **ElevenLabs** (only for voice cloning);
**Apple** (in-app purchases). Each is bound by a data processing agreement.

## Safety
Stories are created with AI and pass through safety filters: input checks, rule-based checks, and an AI moderation
check before anything reaches your child. If a story fails, a pre-approved safe story plays instead. Safety logs mask
the child's name.

## Retention and deletion
We keep data until you delete your account. **Parent Corner › Account › Delete all my data** permanently erases your
account, child profiles, stories, audio files, voice sample, share links and invite records from our servers and your
device. Share links expire after 30 days. Logs without personal data are kept for 12 months.

## Children's privacy (COPPA)
We collect the minimum personal information needed to tell a personalised story, only from the parent, and we never
condition participation on more data. Parents can review, correct and delete their child's information at any time in
Parent Corner, or by emailing us. *[Before a US launch: implement a COPPA-recognised verifiable parental consent method
(e.g. "email plus" or a knowledge-based check) before any child data is sent to the server; the parental gate alone is
not VPC.]*

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (KVKK, GDPR, UK GDPR, US state laws), you can access, correct, delete or export your data
and object to processing. Most of this is available directly in the app; for anything else email destek@masalcik.app.
We respond within 30 days.
